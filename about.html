Our Story

About Us

Date of Birth Calculator is a small collection of free, focused tools built around one simple idea: enter a date, get an accurate answer immediately, without clutter, sign ups, or unnecessary tracking.

What we build

Every calculator on this site runs entirely in your browser. There is no server processing your birth date, no account required, and no data saved anywhere. We built it this way on purpose: a date of birth calculator does not need to know who you are to do its job well.

How we keep results accurate

Each tool uses calendar accurate date math, the kind that correctly handles leap years, variable month lengths, and edge cases like a February 29 birthday. Where a calculator touches an official system, such as the Social Security full retirement age schedule, we base the logic on the publicly published rules and note clearly that it is general information, not personalized advice.

A note on the tools that are just for fun

A couple of tools on this site, the love calculator and the numerology calculator, are included for entertainment and self-reflection. We label them clearly as such. They are fun to play with, but they are not scientific instruments, and we would rather say that plainly than let anyone mistake them for something they are not.

Questions or feedback

If you spot something that looks wrong or have an idea for a calculator we should add, reach out through the contact page.
