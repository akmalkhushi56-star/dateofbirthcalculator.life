What a life path number is

In numerology, your life path number comes from adding together every digit in your date of birth and reducing the total to a single digit, unless it lands on a "master number," 11, 22, or 33, which are traditionally left unreduced. It is treated as a broad personality snapshot, similar in spirit to a zodiac sign, rather than a scientific measurement.

What a name number adds

Your name number comes from assigning each letter of your name a numeric value and reducing the total the same way. Numerologists use it alongside the life path number for a fuller picture, though naturally opinions vary on exactly how much weight to give it.

This tool is meant for entertainment and personal reflection. It is not a scientific or predictive system, and results should be read in that spirit.

Frequently asked questions

How is a life path number calculated from a date of birth?

By adding every digit in the full date of birth together and reducing the sum to a single digit, unless the total is a master number (11, 22, or 33), which is traditionally kept as is.

What is a master number in numerology?

Master numbers, 11, 22, and 33, are considered to carry extra significance in numerology and are usually not reduced further the way other totals are.

Is numerology scientifically accurate?

No. Numerology is not a scientifically validated system. This calculator is provided for entertainment and self-reflection purposes only.

Do I need to enter my name to get a result?

No, the name field is optional. You will always get a life path number from your date of birth, and a name number if you choose to add your name.
