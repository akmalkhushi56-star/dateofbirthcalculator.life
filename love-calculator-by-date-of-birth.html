Frequently asked questions

Is this love calculator scientifically accurate?

No, it is built purely for entertainment. Compatibility in real relationships depends on communication, values, and effort, none of which a formula can measure.

Will I get the same score if I run it again?

Yes, the same two names and dates of birth will always produce the same score, since the calculation is consistent rather than random.

Can I use this for friends or family, not just romantic partners?

Sure, it works with any two names and dates. Just keep in mind it is meant as a fun icebreaker, nothing more.
