When you would use an age difference calculator

Age gaps come up more often than you would think: comparing your age to a sibling's, checking the gap between partners, figuring out how many years separate two coworkers, or working out how old a parent was when their child was born. This tool gives you the exact gap, not a rounded estimate, by comparing the two dates directly.

How the age gap is calculated

The calculator lines up both birth dates and works out the difference the same way you would count on a calendar: full years, then leftover months, then leftover days. It also gives you the total gap in days, weeks, and months, which is useful if you want a single number instead of a mixed unit answer.

Frequently asked questions

How is the exact age difference between two people calculated?

The calculator compares both dates of birth directly and returns the gap in years, months, and days, along with total days, weeks, and months for convenience.

Does it matter which date I enter first?

No. The calculator automatically figures out which date is earlier and labels the older person accordingly.

Can I use this to compare ages at a specific event, not just today?

This version compares birth dates directly, which gives you the permanent age gap between the two people, the same gap that holds true on any date.
