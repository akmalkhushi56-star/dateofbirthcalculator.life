Was 1930 a leap year?

No, 1930 was a standard 365 day year.

What generation is someone born in 1930?

Someone born in 1930 belongs to the Silent Generation generation, and shares their birth year's Chinese zodiac sign, the Horse, with everyone else born in 1930 regardless of the exact month.
