Your life, counted in days

Years are the usual way we measure age, but counting in days gives you a different, sometimes more interesting perspective. Turning 10,000 days old, for example, happens at just under 27 and a half years, well before most people expect it, and it makes for a genuinely fun milestone to celebrate.

This calculator counts the exact number of days between your date of birth and today, then also breaks that down into weeks and hours, and tells you how far away your next big round-number milestone is.

Frequently asked questions

How many days old am I?

Enter your date of birth above and the calculator instantly counts the exact number of days between then and today.

What counts as a days-alive milestone?

This tool flags the next round number milestone, in steps of 1,000 days up to 10,000, then in steps of 10,000 days after that, so you know exactly when to celebrate.

Is the day count exact or an estimate?

It is an exact calendar count, not an estimate, and it correctly accounts for leap years along the way.
