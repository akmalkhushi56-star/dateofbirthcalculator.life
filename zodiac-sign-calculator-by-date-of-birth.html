Two zodiac systems, one date of birth

Your date of birth actually maps to two completely different zodiac systems. The western zodiac, the one most people mean by "star sign," is based on the sun's position on your birth date and runs through twelve signs across the calendar year. The Chinese zodiac instead assigns an animal to your entire birth year, cycling through twelve animals once every twelve years.

This calculator gives you both in one step: your western sign based on the month and day you were born, and your Chinese zodiac animal based on the year.

Note on the Chinese zodiac: the traditional Chinese calendar year does not start on January 1, so people born in January or early February sometimes technically belong to the previous animal year. This calculator uses the calendar year for simplicity, which is accurate for the vast majority of birth dates.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my zodiac sign from my date of birth?

Enter your date of birth above. The calculator checks the month and day against the standard zodiac date ranges and returns your western sign instantly.

What is the difference between a zodiac sign and a Chinese zodiac animal?

A western zodiac sign is based on the exact month and day of birth and changes roughly every month. A Chinese zodiac animal is based on the birth year and changes only once every twelve years.

Why might my Chinese zodiac animal be off by one for a January or February birthday?

The Chinese calendar year starts on a different date each year, usually in late January or February, not January 1. This calculator uses the standard calendar year, which is accurate except for that short window.
